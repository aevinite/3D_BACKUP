## The two things sweeps keep filing wrongly — do not make it three

- **"Dish names don't translate."** Filed as a defect by the guest sweep (2026-08-04) **and** the
  wording sweep (T15, 2026-08-05). It is the owner's decision, made out loud on 2026-08-05:
  **categories and filters translate, dish names and descriptions do not**, and when dish search in
  another language is wanted, `searchAlias` is the answer. `npm run verify:i18n-scope` fails if the
  decision is reversed and **prints it** when it passes.
- **"Seven translations are just the English word."** They are: Pizza, Sushi, Pasta, Protein and
  Burgers are the same word in German or French, and `greeting` is `"BONJOUR"` — French already.
  A mechanical value-diff reports all seven. **Judge the word, not the diff.** The guard keeps a
  separate watch-list, with a reason per entry, for the real leftovers.

## The dictionary, measured independently of the guard

**67 keys × 6 languages (en, de, fr, ar, hi, ko) — none missing, in any language.** Non-Latin
scripts are safe: the animated title splits by GRAPHEME, not by character (`.split("")` once broke
every Hindi word and no text assertion caught it), and the saved-language read is wrapped so a
device that blocks storage still gets a menu.
