## HOW THESE 639 WERE RUN — and why that matters more than the number

Two guards, both re-runnable by anyone, both keyed to the ids in this table:

* **`npm run verify:t25-doors`** — 663 checks with no network, `-live` adds 50 more against a
  running app. **The pure halves of `lib/userAuth.ts` are RUN, not grepped.** The real `loginUser`,
  `verifySecret`, `hashSecret`, `roleSatisfies`, `userFromCookie` and `describeLoginTarget` execute
  against the repo's in-memory stub (`scripts/panel-stubs/sb.mjs`), so a lockout, a disabled
  account, a re-used binned name and the two-restaurants-one-name case are DRIVEN rather than read
  about. That is how `P78747` found item 5, which no amount of reading would have shown.
* **`npm run verify:t25-writes`** — the shipped editor route BUNDLED with esbuild and its
  POST / PATCH / DELETE handlers CALLED, the same harness `verify:manager-gates` uses. **Both
  faults it defends were invisible to a grep** — items 1 and 2 below.

It also **re-executes the 144 rows sweeps #6 and #7 wrote about these files** (`T10.md` ×114,
`T24.md` ×27, `T27.md` ×3) under their ORIGINAL ids. Those rows are marked in place in their own
files; none was renumbered, and none of them regressed.

## WHAT THIS TERRITORY IS ACTUALLY LIKE

The login family is in **very good shape** and it is the most carefully commented code in the repo:
every refusal is a sentence a person can act on, every reason a login failed goes to the admin log
and never to the person, the transient-vs-wrong distinction is honoured at every door, and both
REJECTED decisions (R19's `100vh`, R24's brand marks) are still standing with their comments intact.
Three of the five faults are in the WRITE api, not in the doors.

The one shape worth naming for the next sweep: **all five faults were things a DELETED feature or a
SHARED map left behind**, not things anybody wrote wrong. Auto-settle went and its three reads
stayed; the save path's `TABLES` map was reused by the delete path and carried a fourth kind with
it; a guard's cookie name was invented and nothing ever compared it with the app's. None of that is
visible by reading the handler you are looking at.
