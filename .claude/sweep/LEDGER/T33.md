## How these 50 are re-run

`node scripts/sweep/t33/s9-checks.mjs` — one script, committed, read-only (SQL goes through the
management API with `read_only: true`; the filesystem half only reads). It prints one line per id.
Its SQL transport is `curl`, not `fetch`, on purpose: five sweep terminals share that one endpoint
and it was measured at 4–9 seconds to connect during this run, where Node's undici gives up at a
fixed 10s and tries IPv6 first. A busy endpoint is treated like offline — generous deadline plus
jittered backoff — which is this project's own rule.

Three of the fifty were **sabotaged** rather than trusted: dropping a `SET search_path` from a
DEFINER function turned `P104835` red, appending a bare `CREATE TABLE` turned `P104838` red, and
appending an unguarded `UPDATE settings` turned `P104837` red. All restored.
