  `public/content/starter-menu.json` carries 10 unsplash URLs and `lib/starterMenu.ts` reads it, so a
  brand-new restaurant's first dish photos come from there.
- **P14043 — withdrawn.** "Eight HISTORY docs lost their banner" was my grep looking for
  `⚠️ HISTORY` when the banners are written `⚠️ **HISTORY`. All nine are present.

Three checks in the re-run script were themselves wrong and were fixed rather than believed —
worth recording because each is a trap the next sweep will meet:

- the fence check compared against `origin/main`, **which moved mid-run** (another PR merged), so it
  reported another terminal's files as mine. It must compare against `git merge-base HEAD origin/main`.
- "no secret in `.claude/`" matched the WORD `service_role` in prose. A secret is a VALUE; match one.
- "no CI step reads outside the repo" and "HSTS carries no preload" both matched the long comments
  that EXPLAIN those rules. Judge the `run:` lines and the header VALUE, not the prose around them.
