staff-login limit is 5 per 5 minutes and tripping it alerts the owner about himself.

## How the ids are laid out, and what to do when one drifts

Ids come from ONE counter in load order (`t24-run.mjs` → `nid()`), starting at `P77701`. Two blocks
are generated rather than hand-written — one row per database statement in the half, for scoping and
for bounding — and those are ordered by **(table name, n-th read of that table)**, never by file
position, so inserting a `settings` read moves only the later `settings` rows. The count is locked
per table by `P77903`. **If that row goes red, do not renumber anything: append the new read's rows
at the END of the block and move the number for its table.**
