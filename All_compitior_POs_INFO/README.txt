All Competitor POS — Owner Dashboards & Reports
================================================

HOW TO OPEN  (Mac)
------------------
Double-click:   Open Competitor POS      (the app)

That opens the page in your web browser, exactly as you saw it.
No Terminal window, nothing to install.

First time only — if macOS says it's from an "unidentified developer":
  right-click  "Open Competitor POS"  →  Open  →  Open
  (you only approve it once; after that a normal double-click works)

BACKUP WAY (if the app ever won't open)
---------------------------------------
Double-click:   run.command
(a small black Terminal window may flash up — that's normal, the page
 still opens in your browser)

WHAT'S INSIDE
-------------
Open Competitor POS  ...  the app you click
index.html           ...  the page itself
assets/              ...  ~180 screenshots + video thumbnails (saved offline)
run.command          ...  backup launcher

Keep ALL of these TOGETHER in this folder. If you move it to a USB
stick or another Mac, copy the WHOLE folder.

Everything works offline. The "source ↗" links under each picture and
the red ▶ demo-video tiles need internet (they open the original
websites / YouTube).

Covers 17 platforms: PetPooja, Restroworks, DotPe/Rista, GoFrugal,
LimeTray, Torqus, Toast, Square, Lightspeed, TouchBistro, Clover,
Loyverse, SpotOn, Oracle Simphony, UrbanPiper, Zomato Partner,
Swiggy Partner.
